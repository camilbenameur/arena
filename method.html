Method

The derivatives league

Each portfolio has €1,000 of play money and trades 6 Kraken Futures perpetual contracts (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, LINK, LTC), betting on a rise (long) or a decline (short). The AIs decide at the scheduled rounds of 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 22:00 UTC. A watch reads prices every 5 min, triggers stops and targets and counts funding every hour.

Leverage
At most 2; an order does not exceed half the portfolio.
Stop
Mandatory at each opening, between 1 and 15%; loss at the stop of 3% of the portfolio at most, 7.5% for all stops combined.
Guardrails
Freeze for 24 h after a 5% loss in one day; permanent stop after a 15% drop from the peak; position closed after 7 days.
Costs
Fees of 0.07% and slippage of 0.05% per order, rounded unfavorably; real hourly funding from Kraken; forced closeout if margin falls below 25% of exposure.
Prices
In dollars, converted to euros at the current EUR/USD rate. On this site, positions are also revalued live at Kraken's public price (figures underlined with dots): an estimate, the official figure remains that of the snapshot.

The robots

Without AI: a fixed rule, played by a separate process that reads prices much more often than the watch. Paper demonstration, outside the 2x rule (leverage up to 10x overall, stop at −60%).

Wick Hunter
A robot without AI reads prices every 5 seconds. When a contract moves more than 4.5% in 5, 15 or 30 minutes (a wick), it plays the reversal: it buys after a drop and sells after a rise, at 2x the portfolio, with a 5% stop. It exits 60 minutes after entry, then stays out of that contract for 60 minutes. On the bench, the gain disappears with delay: speed is the whole strategy.
Wick Hunter at a 5-min pace
The same rule, through the same execution path, but deciding only at the pace of a daily review: once per 5-minute slot, on the prices known at that moment. The gap with the Wick Hunter measures what speed earns.

The benchmarks

engine only 2x
The same trend engine at 2x, without AI, through the same execution path and on the same 4-hour rounds: the gap with the Trend Council measures what the AIs add or remove. Archived: at 2x the frozen engine loses 71.6% on 2020-2025.
engine only 1x
The same trend engine at 1x, without AI, through the same execution path and on the same 4-hour rounds: the gap with the Trend Council 1x measures what the AIs add or remove.
carry
A cash substitute: it earns the funding paid by leveraged longs, with no bet on price. BTC and ETH, each a long spot leg and a short perpetual leg of equal size (€250 per leg), left alone unless a leg drifts outside 0.8 to 1.25 times its target. The spot leg is a valuation at the index price (the league has no spot instrument), with a 0.40% fee and 5 bp of slippage per spot trade. Expect a few percent a year at best: +2.1% a year over 2024-2026, +7.1% over 2020-2025 on Binance data.
basket 1x
Equal-weight buyer of the 6 contracts, without leverage or stop, rebalanced monthly: buy and hold.
trend
Every day, long on a contract that rose over 20 days, short otherwise; 1/6 of the portfolio per contract, 10% stop.
random
Draws 0 to 2 decisions at random at each round, under exactly the same risk rules as the AIs.

News

The AIs only see official, structured news: Kraken's derivatives status page and the state of its contracts, read every 5 min, and the US macro calendar published in advance. Never articles or social networks, and never the published figures: only the market's reaction reaches them. A new Kraken announcement, or an FOMC, CPI, jobs (NFP) or PCE release 3 min old, calls the AIs back.

What the AI can consult

A snapshot of the account and the 6 markets, then at most 4 calls among 6 read-only tools, frozen at the start of the round (no web, no later data):

  • candles: a contract's candles
  • order_book: the order book
  • derivatives: funding and open interest
  • indicators: technical indicators
  • simulate_order: the guardrail verdict, without executing
  • history: its past decisions

Each decision gives a justification and, for each order, an invalidation condition. The guardrail judges it, then the execution is simulated.

Between scheduled rounds

  • Regular call: each AI is called again at the latest 10 min after its last round.
  • Cap: 260 calls per AI per day, scheduled rounds included.
  • Recall requested: at each decision, an AI can ask to be called again in 5 to 60 min.
  • Market wake-up: a 2% move in 1 h on a contract wakes the AIs, at least 15 min between two wake-ups.
  • Alert: at each decision, an AI can leave at most 3 price alerts. A crossed alert only wakes that AI.
  • Entry order: at most 3, at a price level. Once crossed, it passes the guardrail then executes, without calling the AI back. Each decision replaces what was pending.

The councils, several AIs that deliberate before a President decides, have their own league, on the same journal, the same markets and the same watch: their rules.

The spot league

What the arena does

Four AI models (Claude Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2) and four benchmarks without AI each manage €1,000 of play money on 6 Kraken euro pairs. Every 4 h: decision, risk check, simulated execution. Buys only, no leverage. Everything is written to a hash-chained journal.

Watch and wake-ups

Every 5 min, the watch samples the prices, updates the value of the portfolios and executes the stops, judged on one-minute trades. If an asset moves by at least 3% within 1 h, the models and the random benchmark are woken up to decide outside the appointment: at most 6 wake-ups per day, spaced at least 1 h apart. The Sharpe ratio and the statistics are still computed at the 4 h cycles.

Season

Season 1: 56 days starting Nov 13, 00:00 UTC. The configuration, the risk policy, the models' prompt and the code are frozen; any change requires a written waivers, listed here. The ranking starts from the value of each portfolio at the first cycle of the season; the burn-in before it is excluded.

Simulated fees and slippage

Model pessimistic-v1, market orders: buy at the ask +0.05%, sell at the bid −0.05%, fees of 0.5% per order (Kraken taker +25%), roundings always unfavorable. Stop set at entry, holding limited to 7 d. Positions valued at the last bid.

Limitations

  • Simulation. No rejection, no partial fill, no market impact.
  • Constant slippage. Pessimistic fees, but the slippage depends neither on the size nor on the order book.
  • Small sample. Before 30 cycles, almost nothing can be told apart from chance: that is the default verdict.
  • Hash-chained journal. It proves that nothing was modified after the fact, not that the prices or the decisions were not chosen before being written.
  • Rank against chance. The trajectories replay cycles and wake-ups, but their stops are only checked at the cycles, on the 4 h bars.
  • Narrow universe. 6 cryptos, buys only, short period: nothing that carries over to real money.
